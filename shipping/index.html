Shipping

Container Shipping, Built For The Archipelago.

From a family-run operator in 1971 to one million containers a year across 39 ports — Tanto moves Indonesia’s cargo by sea, on schedules you can plan around.

What We Move

Cargo Of Every Kind. One Standard Of Care.

01

General Cargo

Containers of every size and type — 20' and 40' standard, flat rack and motor transport — with more than 60,000 in the fleet, properly labelled by commodity and averaging under five years of age.

60,000+ containers Equipment →
02

Reefer Cargo

Every vessel class carries reefer capacity — 18 to 110 plugs per ship — for temperature-sensitive cargo moving between islands, from Surabaya and Jakarta gateways.

18–110 plugs / vessel Fleet →
03

Project & Heavy Lift

A dedicated 605 TEU heavy lift class for machinery, industrial equipment and out-of-gauge cargo that standard services cannot carry.

Heavy lift class Fleet →
04

Dry Bulk — Lumoso Affiliate

PT Lumoso Pratama Line, the dry bulk unit of the Tanto group, operates Handymax/Supramax (55,000 dwt) to Panamax (70,000 dwt) vessels — more than 2 million metric tons a year, from coal and ores to grain.

2M+ t / year chartering@lumososhipping.com

Service Advantages

The Reputation Is The Product.

The backbone of the company rests with its dedicated, customer-focused staff — a well-deserved reputation in the industry as “the multitasketeers”: people who get things done, whether it’s expediting a shipment, getting a responsive rate quote, or collaborating on hard-to-ship special equipment.

With more than 40 years of experience, Tanto operates to the strictest standards of safety and environmental protection. It is not surprising that customers entrust more than three hundred thousand containers to the company every year.

“We’re always thinking on how to improve things.”

Tanto — Service Advantages
  • First in Indonesia to introduce the SMS Release Order and the Online Container Information System
  • First shipping line to carry a container to Bitung — 1992
  • ISM-certified vessels, ISO 9001 & ISO 4500 certified operations
  • 1M+ containers entrusted to Tanto every year
