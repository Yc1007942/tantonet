About Tanto

A Family-Owned Operator Since 1971.

Despite humble beginnings, Tanto has grown to be a recognized player in the shipping industry — committed to consistently delivering the highest levels of performance.

EST. 197160+ VESSELS33,000+ TEU39 PORTS

The Company, In Brief.

Tanto is a family-owned company established in 1971. Today, the company owns a modern fleet of more than 60 container vessels with a total capacity of 33,000+ TEU, serving 39 ports across Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Nusa Tenggara, Maluku and Papua.

Tanto is a company that overcomes all difficulties — committed to being consistent in delivering the highest levels of performance. Safety and security come first at every level of operations, and more than one million containers are entrusted to the company every year.
