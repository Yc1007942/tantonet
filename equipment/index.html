Our Containers

New, Used, Labelled. Always Ready.

Container Handling

Fully Owned. Fully Certified.

Tanto operates fully-owned reach stackers and container forklift trucks — Kalmar and SMV Konecranes — with an array of engineered features built for yard and terminal work.

  • Box-type chassis for stable container handling
  • Load-sensing hydraulics — power-on-demand
  • Electronically controlled low-emission engines
  • Electronic overload protection

All heavy equipment operators are certified and recertified annually through a comprehensive internal training program — so customers receive the level of service they insist upon and deserve.
